Hi, I’m Kilian.

I’m 21 and live in Regensburg, Germany. I like building things across design, engineering, film, and the internet.

Right now, I’m building Avo.

We believe filmmaking is about to become a medium for everyone. As machines take over more of production, far more people will be able to create entire films. Avo is built around one principle: automate the production, preserve the direction.

Before this, I worked at BMW. I left in 2026 to focus full time on building my own things.

Most of my time is spent designing, coding, writing, and exploring what new creative tools can make possible.

I’m always happy to connect.
